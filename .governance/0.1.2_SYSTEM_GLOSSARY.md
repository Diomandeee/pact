# SYSTEM GLOSSARY

The mission is to make prompting discussable mathematically. That requires
words that mean one thing. Every term below is used in exactly this sense
throughout the codebase, the specs, and the writing.

## The engine (L0)

**Type** — the kind of a resource: `M_plan`, `State`, `M_creative`. A newtype
over String so it can never be confused with a node id.

**Bag** — a multiset of typed resources. What a pool holds, what a converter
eats.

**Node** — Pool, Source, Drain, Converter, Gate, Delay. Six kinds, each
expressing something the others cannot.

**Resource connection** — work moves along it. `out(A) == in(B)`.

**State connection** — behaviour is modified along it, no work moves.
Activator, Trigger, Modifier, Inhibitor. This is where cross-layer forcing
lives.

**Conservation** — created equals destroyed plus held, per type. Checked, not
assumed.

## The algebra (L1)

**Combinator** — one of six ways to compose: `▸` sequence, `⊗` broadcast,
`⊕` merge, `↺` feedback, `+` choice, `*` iterate.

**Word** — a chain expressed in combinators. `chain:genesis` was
`⊗ ▸ ✗ ✗ ⊗ ⊕` when scanned from prose and is `▸ ▸ ▸ ▸ ▸` now that it
declares its members.

**Declared members** — a chain's `members:` list in its SOOP-2 block, in
order, with `(a | b)` marking a fan-out. The declaration decides the shape;
the types decide whether the shape is legal. A chain without one is scanned
by first mention, which made its word depend on its prose.

**Refinement** — a specific material satisfies the general one: `M_plan ⇒ M`.
`SKILL_TYPES_v1.md` §4.3 rule 2, honoured in composition since 2026-11-03.
Not honoured in the economy, which stays nominal (P3.6).

**Sanctioned drain** — a type nothing consumes on purpose: `Report` and
`silent` are what a person takes away, the partner of `Subject`, which is
what a person brings and nothing produces. Every other dead end is a defect.

**Protocol** — a predicate on graphs plus typed ports. Validates first,
generates second.

**railpulse** — the canonical cycle, `↺(⊗ ▸ * ▸ ⊕)`. Expand, execute, select,
repeat. Not a primitive; the shape most useful words have.

**Degenerate** — a word missing one or more canonical stages. Every chain in
the corpus is degenerate.

## The analysis (L2)

**Nerve** — the complex whose vertices are tracks and whose simplices are sets
of tracks sharing a named artifact.

**b₀** — connected components. `b₀ > 1` means a plan is a disjoint union
pretending to be a plan.

**b₁** — cycles that do not bound. A dependency loop with no closing
deliverable: everybody busy, nothing built.

**Hardening** — building the artifacts that make the cycles bound. `b₁ → 0`.
Not a checked task list.

**Pressure** — producers over consumers for a type. `M_creative` is 16.5.

**Filtration** — an ordered sequence of graphs plus the maps between them.

**Barcode** — the homology classes with birth and death generations. The
system's actual output.

**Simplicial map** — a compaction that may collapse vertices and may not
create a face absent from the source. A face with no preimage was fabricated.

## Process

**Gate** — a command that passes or fails. Checklist items are ticked by
gates, never by judgement.

**Canary** — a mechanical detector for an invariant. An invariant without one
is a promise.

**Blocked** — the attaching map does not exist. Categorically different from
failed: retrying cannot help, only building the boundary can.

**Cold artifact** — named in a plan, absent on disk. The face is claimed and
not built.

## Deliberately not used

**"Agent"** on its own, without saying which. **"Pipeline"** as a compliment;
here it means a system with no feedback, which is a defect. **"Optimize"**
without a measured before. **"Simply"**, ever.
